Ça fait 10?

Which ones make ten?

Tap a box to say Oui or Non. Ten is two hands.

Read the number, then check

Mme Ashley says a number. You write it. Read the word out loud first, then tap.

Careful with vingt et un, trente et un and quarante et un. Those three take et. All the others take a little dash.

Tous les mots fréquents The thirty words from Mme Ashley's three lists

Bonjour Addie

Thirty little words

These are the words that turn up in every single sentence you read. They do not follow the sound rules, so the trick is to know them by sight — fast, without stopping to sound them out.

September, October and November, all together now.

Les trente mots

Read it, then check

Read the word out loud first. Then tap it to see what it means.

Keep tapping Twelve more until you have seen all thirty.

Quel mot?

Which word fits?

Read the whole sentence first. Then tap the word that belongs in the gap.

Vrai ou faux

Is that really how it is spelled?

Some of these are real words from your lists. Some have the letters jumbled. Tap Vrai for a real one and Faux for a fake.

If you are not sure, picture the word on Mme Ashley's list.

Écrire

Write two sentences

Use at least three of your thirty words in each one. Capital letter, spaces between the words, period at the end.

How this page works

Stars, and the code

STARS AND POCKET MONEY

A whole round correct earns a star. 20 stars is $5. The piggy bank shows what is owed now and what has been earned all year. There is no ceiling — more rounds keep paying.

GROWN-UPS, AT THE FOOT

Behind our four-digit code: give stars for anything at all, take some back, correct the total, record a payment of any amount, and read the dated ledger of every one of those. The digits are deliberately not written here — this page is public and the girls can read it.

Stars are saved in the browser on her device. Open the progress report on that same iPad to see both girls and settle up. The logins on Online Resources are shared school accounts rather than personal ones, and this page is public, so keep the link in the family.
